# Brag Plan: AI-Assisted Dispute Intake

## What is this app?
A dispute-intake tool that lets card-network agents review AI-suggested classifications — labeled by confidence with a next step, not a raw score — while the AI itself never submits a decision; only a logged human action commits.

## The angle
A trailer, not a demo. Open on the cost of the old way — a manual, untraceable process where a missed signal in a regulated decision is a real risk. Turn on the redesign: confidence becomes a label with an action, not a number nobody can act on. Prove it with speed: the agent moves from guess to verified decision in seconds. Close on the guardrail that makes that speed safe: the AI proposes, a person decides, and the decision is the only thing ever logged as final.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Black frame. Small mono type ticks in: "CASE #48213 — DUPLICATE CHARGE CLAIM." Then, slower, heavier: "Every dispute starts the same way." Tension, not slogan.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The old intake: a dense, dark transaction log scrolling under a cursor that keeps stopping and re-checking — the visual of a person losing time to ambiguity.
- The stakes line, isolated on black: "A fast answer with no visible reason is an answer no one uses."
- The cut to light: three real confidence pills (HIGH #047857 / MEDIUM #B54708 / LOW #D92D20) landing one at a time, each paired with its action word (Accept / Corrected / Override).
- The LOW pill triggering an override in real time — reason typed, Confirm pressed — evidence-first, not automation-first.

## Outro / punchline
Three short lines, held one at a time: "The AI proposes." → "A person decides." → "The AI never submits." Then title card: AI-Assisted Dispute Intake — Abhikant Nirbhavane.

## User flow worth showing
Entry: agent opens a case with an ambiguous sub-reason code (LOW confidence, red pill).
Key action: agent reads the evidence, overrides with a logged reason.
Result: Confirm commits the record; it appears in the audit trail as a data point, not just a transaction.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: a trailer for a compliance product — the drama comes from the stakes of a regulated decision, not from spectacle. Big type, wide dark-to-light contrast, restrained motion that earns its scale.
- Interpretation: fewer, longer-held scenes (4-5); dramatic wipes/crossfades between the dark "legacy" half and the light "product" half; type is the hero, UI recreation is the proof.

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support with a low swell
- Music: dark, restrained cinematic bed — sparse low strings/pad through the legacy half, a brightening turn at the cut to product, resolving to a confident low hit on the final line
- Music treatment: starts near-silent under Scene 1, swells through Scene 2, brightens/lifts at Scene 3's cut to light, settles under Scene 4, final low hit under "The AI never submits."
- Music cue guidance: to be detected at composition time (no bundled preset chosen yet); target strong cues at (a) the cut from legacy to product UI, (b) the LOW pill/override moment, (c) the final line "The AI never submits." Sequential pill reveals in Scene 3 should land on alternating beats, not every beat, to protect reading time.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — bed presence may breathe slightly under text holds, nothing flashier
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched — a soft low tone under Scene 1's cursor stalls, a distinct confirm/commit sound on the final Confirm press, no generic whooshes
- Audio-coupled moments: cursor stalling in the legacy log (Scene 1), each confidence pill landing (Scene 3), typed override reason (Scene 3), Confirm press (Scene 4)
- Restraint rule: music never competes with the three outro lines — drop to near-silence under "The AI proposes / A person decides / The AI never submits" so each lands in near-quiet
